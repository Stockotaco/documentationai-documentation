---
title: Pi Thread Not Starting
description: Resolve issues when a pi thread fails to launch.
---

## Symptoms

- Thread status stays at "pending"
- No output logs generated

## Possible Causes

- Insufficient compute quota
- Dataset not available

## Quick Fix

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check Quota">
    ````bash
    curl -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_API_KEY" https://api.mythicdata.com/bridge/quota
    ````
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify Dataset">
    Ensure dataset exists in your workspace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Restart Thread">
    Retry from Bridge console if resources are free.
  </Step>
</Steps>
