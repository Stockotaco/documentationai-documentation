## Overview

Run your first parallel thread in minutes.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Bridge">
    Log in at https://dashboard.mythicdata.com and go to Bridge.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start Pi Thread">
    Click “New Pi Thread”, select a dataset, and hit “Start”.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Monitor Output">
    View logs and results on the dashboard as your thread runs.
  </Step>
</Steps>
